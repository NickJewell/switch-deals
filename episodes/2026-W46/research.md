## 1. FINAL FANTASY VII REBIRTH (Switch 2)
**Pedigree:**
- Square Enix (director Naoki Hamaguchi).
- Released on PS5 on 29 Feb 2024 and on PC in Jan 2025. The Switch 2 and Xbox versions followed on **3 June 2026**.
- It's part two of the Remake trilogy. Remake Intergrade came to Switch 2 in Jan 2026.
- **91.5 GB** download (UK listing). The physical release is a game-key card.
- Includes "Streamlined Progression" (an optional easy mode) and New Game Head Start.
- If you have Intergrade save data, you get the Leviathan and Ramuh materia.

**Reception:** Metacritic (Switch 2) **87** from 53 critics, user score 8.0 (34). For comparison, PS5 scored 92 and the all-platform OpenCritic score is 91.
- "This is easily one of the greatest JRPGs of the last several years, and that's still true of the Switch 2 port." (Nintendo Life, 8/10)
- "It lacks new content, yet this port stands as a definitive way to experience a modern masterpiece." (Vandal, 95)
- "This is the kind of on-the-go experience that was pure science fiction not so long ago." (Digitally Downloaded, 100)
- "…frequent and significant enough to be a noticeable issue." (Nintendo Life, on frame drops)

**This version:**
- Targets 30fps, with no performance mode.
- Docked runs at roughly 540p–1080p dynamic with DLSS. Handheld is around 380p–756p ⚠ (one outlet says 576p).
- Soft image quality in handheld, pop-in in the open world, and drops into the low to mid-20s fps in busy scenes.
- Opinion is split: Notebookcheck's roundup calls it "a mess despite improvements", while COGconnected calls it "smooth and stable".
- No post-launch performance patch has been confirmed.

**Colour:**
- In 2024 Square Enix said Rebirth and FFXVI "did not meet expectations". That pushed it to go multiplatform, which is why the game is on Nintendo now. A later earnings report credited the Switch 2, Xbox and PC versions with lifting HD revenue.
- The original FFVII famously left Nintendo for PlayStation in 1997, and now the sequel-remake runs on a Nintendo handheld.
- The in-game card game Queen's Blood became a fan favourite.
- Nominated for Game of the Year at The Game Awards 2024 (Astro Bot won). ⚠ Best Score and Music win is from memory and unchecked.

**For / skip:** It's for handheld RPG fans who've played Remake, and anyone who missed it on PS5. Skip it if frame drops and blur bother you, or if your storage is nearly full.

**Angles:**
- "It left in '97 and it's come home, all 91 gigs of it."
- The "this shouldn't run on this" port, and where the cracks show.
- Play order: Intergrade first. A deep discount is a good moment to buy both.

**Sources:** [MC critics](https://www.metacritic.com/game/final-fantasy-vii-rebirth/critic-reviews/?platform=nintendo-switch-2) · [MC users](https://www.metacritic.com/game/final-fantasy-vii-rebirth/user-reviews/?platform=nintendo-switch-2) · [Nintendo Life review](https://www.nintendolife.com/reviews/nintendo-switch-2/final-fantasy-vii-rebirth) · [NL round-up](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2026/06/round-up-the-reviews-are-in-for-final-fantasy-vii-rebirth-on-switch-2) · [NL resolution](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2026/05/final-fantasy-vii-rebirths-internal-resolution-detailed-for-switch-2) · [Notebookcheck](https://notebookcheck.net/Final-Fantasy-7-Rebirth-Switch-2-review-calls-performance-a-mess-despite-improvements.1313643.0.html) · [TSA](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2026/06/03/final-fantasy-vii-rebirth-switch-2-review-an-unmissable-portable-rpg/) · [COGconnected](https://cogconnected.com/review/final-fantasy-vii-rebirth-switch-2-review/) · [OpenCritic](https://opencritic.com/game/15136/final-fantasy-vii-rebirth) · [VGC sales](https://videogameschronicle.com/news/final-fantasy-7-rebirth-and-final-fantasy-16-did-not-meet-expectations-says-square-enix) · [PSU patch](https://www.psu.com/news/final-fantasy-vii-rebirth-update-1-005-adds-streamlined-progression-new-game-head-start-full-patch-notes/)

## 2. WWE 2K26 Standard Edition (Switch 2)
**Pedigree:**
- Visual Concepts / 2K. Launched day-and-date on PS5, Xbox, PC and Switch 2.
- Early access for premium editions began 6 Mar 2026. Standard launched 13 Mar.
- CM Punk is the cover star.
- **90.5 GB** download (UK listing). The physical release is a game-key card.
- PEGI 16, with paid random items.

**Reception:** Metacritic (Switch 2) **82** from 6 critics, but the user score is **4.1** from 7 ratings. OpenCritic (all platforms) was 81 at launch and is now around 79.
- "WWE 2K26 once again plays like a dream on the Switch 2." (Loot Level Chill, 90)
- "The year-by-year refinements are paying off." (Cubed3, 80)
- "There's stuttering, frame rate drops, and rendering issues to contend with at times." (Pocket Tactics, 8/10)
- Game Informer called The Island mode "a digital shopping mall". ⚠ Its review may not be of the Switch 2 version.

**This version:**
- VGC's comparison with Series X: "generally a solid performance for less powerful hardware", with slightly softer detail.
- Often hits 60fps, even handheld, but dips in chaotic multi-man matches.
- One Polish outlet says it runs worse than 2K25 did.
- Switch 2 extras: Joy-Con 2 mouse controls for menus and Creations, motion-controlled face paint, the touchscreen, and GameShare.
- Frequent patches (now around 1.20) tuning stamina and reversals and fixing performance.

**Colour:**
- Its launch average was the best for a WWE game since SmackDown vs. Raw 2007.
- First CM Punk cover since WWE '13, with a Showcase built around his career.
- The roster tops 400 wrestlers, a series first. Intergender matches work in every match type, and I Quit, Inferno and 3 Stages of Hell are back.
- Last year 2K25 was criticised for shipping as a code-in-a-box. This year it's a key card, but still about 90 GB.
- VGC called 2K25 on Switch 2 "the best wrestling game on a Nintendo console since at least the N64 era" (No Mercy, WrestleMania 2000).

**For / skip:** It's for wrestling fans who want a near-full-fat 2K game handheld, and for Universe and Showcase players. Skip it if you're short on storage, hate grinding for virtual currency, or already own 2K25.

**Angles:**
- "Best wrestling game on a Nintendo machine since No Mercy?"
- 90 GB on a key card: check your card before you buy.
- The critics-versus-players split, and VC as the villain.

**Sources:** [MC critics](https://www.metacritic.com/game/wwe-2k26/critic-reviews/?platform=nintendo-switch-2) · [MC users](https://www.metacritic.com/game/wwe-2k26/user-reviews/?platform=nintendo-switch-2) · [Pocket Tactics](https://www.pockettactics.com/wwe-2k26-switch-2-review) · [VGC comparison](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/video-how-well-does-wwe-2k26-run-on-switch-2-watch-our-comparison-video) · [Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/review/wwe-2k26/more-money-more-problems) · [Patch history](https://www.perfectly-nintendo.com/wwe-2k26-switch-2-all-the-updates/) · [OpenCritic news](https://opencritic.com/news/27546/wwe-2k26-just-quietly-became-the-highest-rated-wrestling-game-since-the-smackdown-vs-raw-era) · [Key card](https://mynintendonews.com/2026/02/02/wwe-2k26-will-be-game-key-card-rather-than-code-in-a-box/)

## 3. HOT WHEELS UNLEASHED (Switch)
**Pedigree:**
- Milestone of Milan (the MotoGP and Ride studio), under licence from Mattel. Built in Unreal Engine 4.
- Released on all platforms on 30 Sept 2021.
- 68 cars in the base game. 2-player split-screen and 12-player online.
- DLC is sold separately: Hot Wheels Pass Vol. 1–3 and a GOTY upgrade.
- 7.9 GB download.

**Reception:** Metacritic (Switch) **73** from 9 critics, user score 7.2.
- "Probably the best Hot Wheels game to date." (Nintendo Life, 7/10)
- "One of the best track editors I've ever seen." (App Trigger)
- "Ultimately fails to amount to anything beyond basic races and time trials." (Nintendo World Report)

**This version:**
- 30fps, "extremely consistent" (NWR). Solid both docked and handheld (Nintendo Life).
- The track builder was **missing at launch on Switch** and was patched in days later.
- Long loading times of around 30 seconds or more.
- On Switch 2 it runs the same as on Switch: no patch and no boost.

**Colour:**
- Milestone pitched the game to Mattel in 2018 without expecting a yes. It was the first console Hot Wheels game since 2013.
- The cars are 1:1 replicas of the real die-casts.
- A sim-racing studio rebuilt its physics for loops and stunts.
- It became **Milestone's fastest-selling game**: 1M copies by Dec 2021, then 2M and 8M players by Apr 2023. It reached No. 4 in the UK chart at launch.
- The sequel, Turbocharged, followed in Oct 2023.
- Nostalgia: orange track and a bedroom-floor setting, much like Micro Machines on the PS1 or N64.

**For / skip:** It's for kids, die-cast nostalgics and track tinkerers. Skip it if you want variety (there are five environments and a thin career), or buy Turbocharged or Mario Kart instead.

**Angles:**
- The sim studio's toy car became its biggest hit.
- The track editor is the real star.
- "Which car did you have as a kid?"

**Sources:** [MC critics](https://www.metacritic.com/game/hot-wheels-unleashed/critic-reviews/?platform=nintendo-switch) · [MC users](https://www.metacritic.com/game/hot-wheels-unleashed/user-reviews/?platform=nintendo-switch) · [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/reviews/nintendo-switch/hot_wheels_unleashed) · [NWR](https://nintendoworldreport.com/review/58508) · [Nintendo US](https://www.nintendo.com/us/store/products/hot-wheels-unleashed-switch/) · [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot_Wheels_Unleashed) · [Gematsu sales](https://www.gematsu.com/2023/04/hot-wheels-unleashed-sales-top-two-million)

## 4. Grand Theft Auto: The Trilogy – The Definitive Edition (Switch)
**Pedigree:**
- Rockstar Games, adapted by Grove Street Games in Unreal Engine.
- Bundles GTA III (2001), Vice City (2002) and San Andreas (2004), all originally on PS2.
- Switch download released 11 Nov 2021. The cartridge followed in Feb 2022 and still requires a download.
- PEGI 18. File size ⚠ unknown.

**Reception:**
- Metacritic (Switch) ⚠ **46** from 14 critics, with a user score of ⚠ **0.9** from 784 ratings. These come from an undated search snippet because the page itself wouldn't load.
- At launch the user score sank to **0.5**, one of the worst ever on Switch.
- OpenCritic (all platforms): 54, with 12% of critics recommending.
- "A poor port, a shoddy, stuttery, low resolution mess full of bugs, glitches, audio problems and more besides." (Nintendo Life, 4/10, at launch)
- "This is a trilogy that should run beautifully on Switch, there can be no excuses." (Nintendo Life)
- "Barring a miraculous future update, you probably shouldn't buy [it] on Nintendo Switch." (VGC, 2/5)

**This version:**
- At launch, Digital Foundry rated Switch the "bottom tier" version. GTA III ran at about 648p docked and 480p handheld, with San Andreas and Vice City often below 25fps.
- **Patch 1.06 (Feb 2022)** was the big fix. Nintendo Life's 2023 revisit found a "near-steady 30fps", a sharper image and faster loading. Clipping, audio bugs, crashes and stuttering in San Andreas remain.
- Update 1.112 (Nov 2024) added "Classic Lighting".
- ⚠ Reportedly steadier on Switch 2 through backwards compatibility, but that's second-hand.

**Colour:**
- Rockstar delisted the originals before launch. After the backlash it apologised and restored them on PC.
- The Rockstar Launcher went down for about three days at launch.
- The 2024 patch removed Grove Street Games from the credits, and its CEO objected publicly.
- Several licensed radio songs are missing.
- These three games never appeared on N64 or GameCube, so this is the 3D-era trilogy's Nintendo debut.

**For / skip:** It's for PS2-era nostalgics who want all three games in their pocket, especially on Switch 2. Purists and anyone who can play elsewhere should skip it.

**Angles:**
- The comeback: from 0.5 to "acceptable" five years later.
- PS2 nostalgia: the radio stations, Tommy, CJ, and what got lost along the way.
- The studio that was airbrushed out of its own credits.

**Sources:** [OpenCritic](https://opencritic.com/game/12230/grand-theft-auto-the-trilogy-the-definitive-edition) · [NL review](https://nintendolife.com/reviews/nintendo-switch/grand-theft-auto-the-trilogy-the-definitive-edition) · [NL revisit](https://nintendolife.com/features/talking-point-has-grand-theft-auto-the-trilogy-the-definitive-edition-on-switch-improved-since-launch) · [DF bottom tier](https://nintendolife.com/news/2021/11/digital-foundry-continues-gta-trilogy-analysis-sets-switch-as-bottom-tier-version) · [DF resolution](https://mynintendonews.com/2021/11/16/digital-foundry-grand-theft-auto-3-on-switch-runs-at-roughly-648p-docked-and-480p-handheld/) · [VGC review](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/review/gta-trilogy-definitive-edition-nintendo-switch/) · [0.5 user score](https://nintendosoup.com/gta-trilogy-for-switch-lands-a-0-5-user-score-on-metacritic-one-of-the-worst-ever-for-the-console) · [1.112](https://mynintendonews.com/2024/11/14/grand-theft-auto-trilogy-on-nintendo-switch-updated-to-version-1-112/) · [Credit removed](https://videogameschronicle.com/news/grand-theft-auto-trilogy-removes-grove-street-games-credit-following-major-graphical-update) · [Rockstar apology](https://www.nme.com/news/rockstar-sorry-for-gta-the-trilogy-as-original-games-return-to-pc-3100248)

## 5. Deponia Collection (Switch)
**Pedigree:**
- Daedalic Entertainment of Hamburg.
- Includes **Deponia, Chaos on Deponia, Goodbye Deponia and Deponia Doomsday**, originally released on PC between 2012 and 2016.
- The collection came out on 21 Apr 2022. It's four separate downloads sold as a bundle, about 7.6 GB in total.
- PEGI 12. Marketed as "over 50 hours".

**Reception:** Metacritic has **no critic reviews of the Collection**. The 2019 standalone Switch *Deponia* scored **64** from 6 critics, and most of those reviews complained about the price.
- "Deponia is a fine example of the genre." (Nintendo Life, 6/10, 2019)
- "The puzzles… are plentiful and well-constructed." (Nintendo Life)
- "The pricing for Deponia on Nintendo Switch is a huge head-scratcher." (COGconnected, 63)
- "Sharp, pretty and quirky-as-hell." (Kotaku, on the PC original)

**This version:** ⚠ These points come from reviews of the 2019 single releases:
- No touchscreen support, even in handheld.
- No free cursor; hotspots light up as Rufus walks near them.
- A/B selection that behaves inconsistently, and minor audio bugs.
- "Looks amazing, especially when played in docked mode." (Nintendo Life)
- No Switch 2 enhancements.

**Colour:**
- In 2019 Switch owners paid about £30 for the first game alone, while PS4 and Xbox got all four for the same money.
- The series had sold 2.2M copies by 2016, mostly in deep discounts. Daedalic admitted "full price sales were a little part of that number".
- Rufus is famously unlikeable, and fans still argue about Goodbye Deponia's ending.
- Daedalic closed its in-house development after *Gollum*. The series continues as *Surviving Deponia*, a PC survival-crafting spin-off (no Switch announcement).

**For / skip:** It's for fans of LucasArts-style comedy adventures. Skip it if you want touch controls or can't stand a selfish lead.

**Angles:**
- Redemption: from full price for one game to the whole quadrilogy for a few quid.
- Rufus: loveable rogue, or just a git?
- The point-and-click studio that bet on Gollum.

## 6. WILD HEARTS S (Switch 2)
**Pedigree:**
- Omega Force (the Dynasty Warriors team) / Koei Tecmo.
- The original Wild Hearts came out in Feb 2023 on PS5, Xbox and PC, published by EA Originals.
- The S version is a **Switch 2 exclusive**, released 25 Jul 2025. It includes all the post-launch beasts and modes.
- Adds **four-player co-op** (up from three) and doesn't need an EA account. No crossplay.
- 33.4 GB download.

**Reception:** Metacritic (Switch 2) **75** from 27 critics, user score 7.1. OpenCritic 80, with 56% of critics recommending.
- "A satisfying, if unspectacular, addition to the Switch 2's early library." (Nintendo Life, 7/10)
- "This one might have benefited from a bit more time in the oven." (Nintendo Life)
- "It still delivers a compelling monster-hunting experience for newcomers." (COGconnected)

**This version:**
- Roughly 45–60fps docked and about 30fps handheld, with dips during transitions.
- Fuzzy resolution and flat textures. No DLSS or HDR.
- TechRadar: a "noticeable visual downgrade" compared with PS5. One demo comparison found it targets 60fps where Series S targets 30, but at a lower resolution.
- Easier early bosses, and monsters flee less often.

**Colour:**
- Producer Takuto Edagawa said Switch 2 "can be thought as closer to the Series S."
- ⚠ EA reportedly ended support seven months after launch. Koei Tecmo then brought the game back as its own release.
- The PC launch in 2023 was notorious for poor performance.
- The hook is Karakuri building, best described as Fortnite meets Monster Hunter in a version of feudal Japan.
- Reviewers repeatedly call it the "underrated Monster Hunter".

**For / skip:** It's for Monster Hunter fans who missed it, and for co-op groups. Skip it if frame-rate dips bother you or you've already played it elsewhere.

**Angles:**
- The game EA abandoned and Koei Tecmo rescued.
- "Closer to a Series S": does the port back that up?
- Monster Hunter's under-loved rival gets a fourth co-op slot.

**Sources:** [MC critics](https://www.metacritic.com/game/wild-hearts-s/critic-reviews/?platform=nintendo-switch-2) · [OpenCritic](https://opencritic.com/game/18983/wild-hearts-s) · [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/reviews/nintendo-switch-2/wild-hearts-s) · [Koei comparison](https://www.koeitecmoeurope.com/wild-hearts-s/uk/comparison/) · [Series S quote](https://www.theshortcut.com/p/switch-2-graphics-are-closer-to-an-xbox-series-s) · [Wccftech](https://wccftech.com/nintendo-switch-2-wild-hearts-s-comparison-surpassing-xbox-series-s/) · [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/gaming/nintendo/wild-hearts-s-review) · [EA support](https://pushsquare.com/news/2023/09/rumour-wild-hearts-support-is-being-slashed-just-seven-months-after-release)

## 7. The Talos Principle: Deluxe Edition (Switch)
**Pedigree:**
- Croteam of Zagreb (the *Serious Sam* studio) / Devolver Digital.
- Originally on PC in Dec 2014.
- The Switch version was released on 10 Dec 2019, revealed in an Indie World showcase and out the same day.
- Includes the base game plus the **Road to Gehenna** DLC. 3.7 GB.
- On Switch 2 it runs the same as on Switch.

**Reception:** Metacritic (Switch) **83** from 4 critics, user score ⚠ 8.2 (it may be mixed up with the PS4 figure). For comparison, PC scored 85 and PS4 88.
- "Utterly entrancing and highly recommended." (Nintendo Life, 9/10)
- "I can't recommend this game enough." (Pure Nintendo, 9/10)
- "Performance smooths things out and works well enough in portable mode, but docked play is a hot mess." (TheGamer, 3.5/5)

**This version:**
- Has a Performance/Graphics toggle.
- Frame drops when docked.
- Long loading, small text in handheld, and fiddly aiming.
- No technical analysis found.

**Colour:**
- It started as *Serious Sam 4* prototyping. A gun that jammed forcefields made puzzles "too cerebral for Sam", and Croteam called the result "the game that we never planned to make".
- Written by Tom Jubert and Jonas Kyratzes.
- An IGF 2015 finalist.
- A game about AI consciousness from 2014 lands differently in 2026.
- **Talos Principle 2 (2023) and the remake Reawakened (Apr 2025) are not on Switch**, so this is the only way to play Talos on a Nintendo console.

**For / skip:** It's for *Portal* and *The Witness* fans. Skip it if you play docked only and hate frame dips, or if you can play Reawakened on PS5 or PC.

**Angles:**
- The headless-kamikaze studio made a philosophy game.
- AI consciousness in 2014 versus 2026.
- "Best way to play Talos on Nintendo, because it's the only way. Play it handheld."

**Sources:** [MC critics](https://www.metacritic.com/game/the-talos-principle-deluxe-edition/critic-reviews/?platform=nintendo-switch) · [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/reviews/switch-eshop/talos_principle_deluxe_edition) · [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/talos-principle-deluxe-edition-review-nintendo-switch/) · [Croteam](https://www.croteam.com/the-talos-principle-is-5-years-old-launches-on-nintendo-switch/) · [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Talos_Principle) · [Deku Deals](https://www.dekudeals.com/items/the-talos-principle-deluxe-edition)

## 8. RollerCoaster Tycoon 3 Complete Edition (Switch)
**Pedigree:**
- Frontier Developments (David Braben's studio). The original came out on PC in Nov 2004.
- The Complete Edition was released on 24 Sept 2020 and includes the **Soaked!** and **Wild!** expansions: 300+ rides, 500 scenery pieces and a sandbox mode.
- 2.8 GB.
- The eShop now lists **Atari** as publisher because Frontier sold the rights back to Atari for $7M in Mar 2024.

**Reception:** Metacritic (Switch) **72** from 18 critics, none negative. User score 7.1. The 2004 PC original scored 81.
- "A triumph of port work." (Cubed3)
- "A generously priced package that's hard to knock." (Nintendo Life, 8/10)
- "Even after 16 years… still fun, engaging, and addictive." (TheGamer)
- "Disappointing visuals and cumbersome controls aside…" (Nintendo Insider)

**This version:**
- Light on the hardware, with slight stutter in big parks (inherited from the original).
- Controls divide people. Twin radial menus sit on the shoulder buttons, with a free cursor on the right stick.
- No remapping and no touchscreen.
- ⚠ Players say the scenario editor is missing.
- On Switch 2 it runs the same as on Switch.

**Colour:**
- It was the first fully 3D RollerCoaster Tycoon. **CoasterCam** let you ride your own creations, which was huge in 2004.
- Chris Sawyer was only a consultant on it.
- **Frontier sued Atari in 2017 over about $2.2M in unpaid royalties.** The game vanished from Steam and GOG in 2018, then returned as this Complete Edition.
- 10M+ sales by 2015.
- Its spiritual successor is Planet Coaster. Atari's own RCT World flopped.
- Guests never die, but crashed coaster cars explode.

**For / skip:** It's for PC-era nostalgics who want a deep theme-park sandbox on the sofa. Skip it if you hate managing things with a pad; the PC version with a mouse is kinder.

**Angles:**
- The royalties row that pulled it off sale, and this edition as the peace deal.
